Any-Meal FAQ

Q. What is Any-Meal? A. Any-Meal recommends one nearby restaurant based on your current location, trusting chance instead of ratings.
Q. How is the recommendation chosen? A. The recommendation is chosen at random.
Q. What happens if I deny location access? A. If location access is unavailable, Any-Meal uses the default area.
Q. Does Any-Meal store my location or personal information? A. Any-Meal does not store your location or personal information.
